Our story

A showroom in the heart of Upper Bazar.

From the Prime Rose Building on Commercial Road, Opticare Optical House has built its name one customer at a time — through a wide collection of frames and sunglasses, careful eye testing, and service that people go out of their way to mention.

Whether it's a family choosing school glasses, a professional trying progressives for the first time, or a tourist who has broken their only pair, our approach is the same: listen, advise honestly and fix the problem quickly.
